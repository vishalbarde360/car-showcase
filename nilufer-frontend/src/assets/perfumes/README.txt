Tips
- Best: transparent PNG/WebP, bottle straight on, about 800 x 1200 px (any size works).
- A JPG with a plain light background also works (it is blended into the beige).
- Any photo that is missing automatically falls back to the drawn bottle.
- Use photos you own or that are licensed for commercial use (your own shoot,
  or a free-licence site like Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay). Bottles with another
  brand's logo on them are not a good fit for a Nilufer product page.
